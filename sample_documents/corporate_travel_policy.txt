# ACME GLOBAL - CORPORATE TRAVEL & REIMBURSEMENT POLICY (2026)

## 1. Daily Meal & Incidental Allowance (Per Diem)
Employees traveling on authorized company business are eligible for a daily meal allowance capped at $85 per day ($20 for breakfast, $25 for lunch, and $40 for dinner). Alcohol purchases are non-reimbursable under standard business expense guidelines. Receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding $25.

## 2. Flight & Train Transportation Rules
All flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy or Premium Economy class. Business class travel is permitted only for international intercontinental flights exceeding 8 consecutive hours with advance written approval from the Vice President. Travel must be booked at least 14 days in advance through the company travel portal to secure enterprise discount rates.

## 3. Lodging & Hotel Caps
Hotel accommodations are reimbursed up to $220 per night in standard tier cities and up to $350 per night in high-cost metro areas (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich). Any room upgrades, spa charges, or in-room pay-per-view movies are the personal responsibility of the employee.

## 4. Ground Transportation & Mileage Reimbursement
Employees using personal vehicles for business travel will be reimbursed at $0.67 per mile, in alignment with federal standard mileage rates. Rental cars require prior approval and must be intermediate or compact sedan tier. Rideshare services (Uber, Lyft) and public transit are encouraged for airport-to-hotel travel.

## 5. Expense Report Submission Deadline
All travel expense reports and itemized receipts must be submitted through the finance portal within 15 business days following the conclusion of the trip. Failure to submit receipts within 30 days may result in delayed or denied reimbursement.
